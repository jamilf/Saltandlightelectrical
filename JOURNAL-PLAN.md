# Journal plan, October 2026 to September 2027

Twenty entries, about one every two to three weeks. Every one is already in `content/journal/` as a draft with its planned date.

- **Ready** means it's written and illustrated, and it passes the check. It's general information and describes nothing you've done, so it only needs you to read it aloud.
- **Yours** means it's about your own experience, so it's an outline with `[[JAMIL: ...]]` gaps and room for your own photo. Rule: never invent experiences.

To publish one, read it aloud, delete its `draft: true` line, change `date:` to the day it goes up if that's different, then build, check and push (or ask Claude to).

## Before publishing a ready entry

- Read it aloud. Change anything that doesn't sound like you. Keep the facts as they are unless you've checked a better source.
- The two entries that quote NSW rules, on supervision and on paperwork, and the one on licences were checked against the NSW Government pages on 30 September 2026. Open the linked page again before publishing, in case the rules have changed.
- The licences entry shows the road to launch as it looked in September 2026. If a stage has changed by then, ask for the picture to be redrawn.

## Your photos

- Save the photo in `content/journal/media/` with a short name, like `first-tools.jpg`.
- Put it on its own line in the post, with a description and a caption:
  `![Screwdrivers and pliers laid out on a bench](media/first-tools.jpg "My first tools, before the first day.")`
- Keep `photos: own` in the front matter, or `photos: employer-approved` for anything from work.
- No faces, names, addresses, house numbers or number plates, and nothing from a job site without your employer's OK. The check fails any photo that still has GPS location data in it. Most phones can turn location off in the camera settings.

## Photos from other people

The ready entries use openly licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr, found through Openverse. Each one was checked for its licence, cropped or resized, and saved again without any metadata. Each entry credits its photos by author, licence and source in a "Photo credits" section, which the check requires for `photos: licensed`. Two of them (tool bag and earth rods) are only moderate matches for their topics; swap them for your own photos if you take something better.
