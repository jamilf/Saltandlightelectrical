About

I'm Jamil Flores. I'm looking for an electrical apprenticeship in Western Sydney, and building Salt and Light Electrical in public as I learn the trade.

{{#if person.photo}}{{/if}}

From IT to the trade

I spent about eight years in IT operations and application support, keeping the systems businesses rely on up and running. It taught me to write down what I did and why, and to stay calm when something breaks at the worst possible moment.

I wanted to work with my hands on things people can see and depend on, and one day to run a business of my own. The electrical trade offers both.

In early 2026 I finished a Certificate II in Electrotechnology, the course that gets people ready for an electrical apprenticeship. Now I'm looking for one, and the IT habits are coming with me.

Why the name

The name comes from Matthew 5:13-16, where Jesus calls his followers the salt of the earth and the light of the world.

Salt keeps things from going bad. Light shows things as they really are. For an electrician, that means work that lasts and honesty about what's behind the wall.

My faith matters to me, and it's why the business has this name. You don't need to share it to follow along. Everyone is welcome here.
