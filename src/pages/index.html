Why the name

The name comes from Matthew 5:13-16. Salt keeps things from going bad, and light shows things as they really are. For an electrician, that means work that lasts and honesty about what's behind the wall.

More about the name

What I'm building

Salt and Light Electrical is planned to open in {{ site.launchYear }}, once I hold an electrical contractor licence. It will focus on commercial and technical electrical work.

Two promises sit under it. Work that's done once and done properly. And clear records of what was done and why, so whoever works on it next isn't left guessing.

Until then I'm not offering electrical work of any kind. This site is the record of getting there.
