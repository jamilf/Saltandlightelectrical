Road to launch

This is the plan, drawn as a single-line diagram. That's the simple circuit drawing electricians use to show how power gets from where it starts to what it runs.

Each stage is a switch. When I finish one, its switch closes and the power gets a step closer to the lamp at the end. The lamp is Salt and Light Electrical. It stays off until the business opens, planned for {{ site.launchYear }}.
